# AR Portal – Marble Mountain Experience

## Project Overview

AR Portal – Marble Mountain Experience is an undergraduate augmented reality project developed using **Unity and Blender**.

The project was designed to create an immersive virtual environment inspired by Marble Mountain, allowing users to experience a digitally recreated location through an AR application.

The development involved 3D environment creation, asset integration, scene composition, and preparing the completed virtual environment for use in augmented reality.

## Development and Implementation

### 1. 3D Environment Development

- Developed a virtual environment inspired by Marble Mountain.
- Used Blender and Unity to create and assemble the 3D environment.
- Constructed and arranged architectural structures and environmental elements.
- Adjusted the positioning, rotation, and scaling of objects to maintain consistent proportions.
- Organized the environment into connected areas to support the virtual experience.

### 2. Asset Creation and Integration

- Worked with 3D models and environmental assets to construct the virtual setting.
- Imported 3D assets into Unity for scene assembly.
- Configured object placement and spatial relationships within the environment.
- Combined individual models into a cohesive virtual scene.

### 3. Materials and Lighting

- Applied materials and textures to architectural and environmental surfaces.
- Configured scene lighting to improve visibility and the visual appearance of the environment.
- Adjusted the presentation of 3D objects to create a consistent atmosphere.

### 4. AR Portal Integration

- Prepared the completed 3D environment for integration into an augmented reality application.
- Integrated the Unity environment into the AR experience.
- Designed the experience around the concept of an AR portal connecting the user's physical surroundings with a virtual Marble Mountain environment.
- Combined 3D scene development with augmented reality presentation.

### 5. Unity Scene Assembly

- Assembled the virtual environment within the Unity Editor.
- Organized scene objects and architectural components.
- Adjusted transforms and scene composition.
- Prepared the environment for integration and testing within the AR application.
